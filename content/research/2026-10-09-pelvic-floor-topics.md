## 1. Constipated Despite Fiber? Your Pelvic Floor May Be Working Backwards

Status: complete at `content/articles/25-constipation-pelvic-floor-dysfunction.md`.

Keyword: `constipation pelvic floor dysfunction`. Category: Science & Safety.

Angle: constipation is not always a fiber problem. Pelvic floor dyssynergia can prevent the outlet muscles from relaxing at the correct moment. Explain coordination versus strength, assessment, and therapist-guided biofeedback.

Sources:

- [North Tees NHS: Obstructive Defaecation Syndrome](https://www.nth.nhs.uk/resources/obstructive-defaecation-syndrome-ods-also-known-as-anismus/)
- [American College of Gastroenterology: Constipation and Defecation Problems](https://gi.org/guidelines/clinical-practice-gi/constipation)
- [ASCRS: Evaluation and Management of Chronic Constipation, 2024](https://www.ascrsu.com/ascrs/view/ASCRS-Toolkit/2851100/all/Evaluation_and_Management_of_Chronic_Constipation__2024_)
- [NIDDK: Treatment for Constipation](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/constipation/treatment)

Guardrails: symptoms do not diagnose dyssynergia; do not prescribe harder Kegels for constipation; retain red flags and assessment advice.

## 3. Can Kegels Fix Erectile Dysfunction? The Evidence Is Complicated

Status: complete at `content/articles/27-kegels-erectile-dysfunction.md`.

Keyword: `kegels for erectile dysfunction`. Category: Science & Safety.

Angle: the anatomy is plausible and one small older trial is encouraging, but a newer meta-analysis did not find a significant effect in its PFMT subgroup. Explain the disagreement without making a promise.

Sources:

- [Dorey et al.: randomized trial, 2004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1324914/)
- [Systematic review of physiotherapy interventions](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38596771/)
- [Chen et al.: exercise and ED meta-analysis, 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38937909/)
- [NIDDK: Symptoms and Causes of Erectile Dysfunction](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/urologic-diseases/erectile-dysfunction/symptoms-causes)

Guardrails: ED can be vascular, neurological, hormonal, medication-related, or psychological. Kegels are a possible adjunct, not a universal replacement for assessment and treatment.

## 4. Leak When You Cough or Lift? Try the Pelvic Floor "Knack"

Status: researched brief; full article not written.

Keyword: `pelvic floor knack men`. Category: Technique.

Angle: explain why coughs, sneezes, standing, and lifting provoke stress leakage; teach a brief lift before pressure and a full release afterward; distinguish a functional response from an all-day clench.

Sources:

- [Oxford University Hospitals: Pelvic Floor Muscles for Men](https://www.ouh.nhs.uk/media/in3ekdyl/74150guide.pdf)
- [University Hospital Southampton: Pelvic Floor Exercises](https://www.uhs.nhs.uk/Media/UHS-website-2019/Patientinformation/Respiratory/Pelvic-floor-exercises-respiratory-3593-PIL.pdf)
- [University Hospitals Plymouth: Pelvic Floor Health](https://www.plymouthhospitals.nhs.uk/display-pil/pil-pelvic-floor-health-4046)

Guardrails: the movement is a lift, not bearing down; repeated or heavy leakage needs assessment; do not promise an instant cure.
