# How Long Do Kegels Take to Work for Women?

> **Quick answer:** Some women notice better awareness or fewer leaks within six weeks, but a fair trial of pelvic floor muscle training is usually measured in months. NICE recommends at least three months of supervised training for stress or mixed urinary incontinence. Results depend on the symptom, technique, consistency, childbirth history, menopause, surgery, and whether the muscles are weak or overactive.

## What May Change First

The first improvement may be coordination: finding a lift without tightening the thighs, buttocks, or abdomen and releasing completely afterward. That skill can improve before strength or symptoms change.

Track a relevant outcome: fewer leaks with coughing, less urgency, better bowel control, or reduced heaviness. Do not judge progress only by how hard the squeeze feels.

## A Realistic Timeline

**Weeks 1–6:** technique and awareness may improve. Some women notice early bladder changes.

**Months 2–3:** regular, correctly targeted training may begin to change strength, endurance, and leakage. This is a useful review point, not a deadline.

**Months 3–6:** progress can continue, especially when symptoms have been present for a long time or the starting contraction was weak.

## Why Results Differ

Kegels help only when the programme matches the problem. Leakage can coexist with prolapse, bladder urgency, tissue changes, constipation, pain, or poor relaxation. An overactive pelvic floor may need down-training and coordination rather than more squeezing.

Technique also matters. Breath-holding and pushing down increase pressure instead of producing a lift. A pelvic health physiotherapist can check both contraction and release.

## When to Ask for Help

Seek an assessment if you cannot feel the muscles, symptoms worsen, exercise is painful, you feel a vaginal bulge, or there is no meaningful change after a consistent three-month trial. Urinary burning, blood, fever, or sudden symptoms need medical assessment rather than exercise adjustment.
