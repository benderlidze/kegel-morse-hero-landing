## Best Actually Usable Free Option: Kegel Trainer PFM Exercises

[Kegel Trainer PFM Exercises](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kegel-trainer-pfm-exercises/id578148339) is free to download and its listing explicitly says it is free to use. It includes short guided sessions, daily reminders, achievements, streaks, and visual, audio, or vibration cues.

The catch is that it is not a strictly subscription-free product. An optional auto-renewing Pro level is offered inside the app.

Choose it if you want:

- A basic timer before deciding whether to pay.
- Sessions lasting roughly 30 seconds to three minutes.
- Discreet vibration guidance.
- Reminders and simple streak tracking.

Skip it if seeing an optional subscription at all is a deal-breaker or if a clinician has prescribed a highly specific rehabilitation routine.

## Best One-Time Upgrade: Squeeze Time for Men

[Squeeze Time for Men](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/squeeze-time-for-men-kegel/id1395804535) is free to download. Its US listing shows one Premium in-app purchase at $4.99 rather than a recurring plan.

The listing highlights custom exercises, reminders, programs, statistics, a calendar, Apple Watch support, and social features. That makes it the most direct match for someone who wants to try an app free and then unlock more without adding another monthly charge.

There are tradeoffs. The listing says the app may use location for reminders, and its privacy label includes tracking and several categories of data not linked to identity. Check the current privacy section before enabling location or community features.

## Best Clinician-Oriented One-Time Purchase: Squeezy Men

[Squeezy Men](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/squeezy-men/id929618748) costs $4.99 upfront in the US store. It was designed by chartered NHS physiotherapists specializing in men’s health and supports customizable plans, a professional mode, a bladder diary, progress graphs, and visual and audio prompts.

It is not free, but it is the clearest current choice for a one-time purchase with no subscription shown in the listing.

Squeezy Men makes the most sense when a pelvic floor physiotherapist has already given you a program. Its professional mode is more relevant in that situation than gamification or a large content library.

## Where Kegel Morse Hero Fits

[Kegel Morse Hero](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kegel-morse-hero-pelvic-floor/id6761460873) is free to download and uses Morse-code rhythms, visual animation, and haptic feedback to guide short contractions and longer holds.

It is not accurate to call it a no-subscription app. The current US listing shows weekly, monthly, and annual plans. It also shows a $29.99 Lifetime Premium purchase, which avoids recurring billing after the one-time payment.

Choose it if the main problem is that ordinary countdown timers become boring. Do not choose it because you need diagnosis, biofeedback, or a clinician-configured post-surgical plan; it does not provide those things.

## What “No Subscription” Should Mean Before You Tap Buy

Check these four details in the store listing and the purchase screen:

1. **Is the download free, or is the useful part free?** A free install can still lead directly to a paywall.
2. **Is the payment one-time?** Look for words such as “Premium” or “Lifetime,” then confirm that the purchase is not described as auto-renewing.
3. **Is there a trial?** A trial may convert to a weekly, monthly, or annual plan unless cancelled.
4. **Can you restore the purchase?** A lifetime or one-time unlock should normally be restorable through the same store account.

The App Store purchase sheet shown immediately before confirmation is the final price to trust.

## Do You Need a Paid Kegel App at All?

You may not need one if you can already perform the correct contraction and follow a clinician-approved routine with a normal timer.

Paying can make sense when an app solves a specific adherence problem:

- You forget sessions without reminders.
- You lose count during holds and rests.
- Haptic cues let you train without watching the screen.
- Custom programming reproduces instructions from a physiotherapist.
- A more engaging format keeps you consistent.

An app cannot determine whether your pelvic floor is weak, overactive, painful, or poorly coordinated. Stop and get assessed if training causes pelvic pain, urinary hesitancy, worsening urgency, or difficulty relaxing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a completely free Kegel app for men?

Kegel Trainer PFM Exercises says it is free to use, with an optional Pro subscription. The free experience and paywall can change, so check the current purchase screen before relying on a feature.

### Which men’s Kegel app has a one-time payment?

The US App Store currently shows Squeeze Time Premium at $4.99, Squeezy Men at $4.99 upfront, and Kegel Morse Hero Lifetime Premium at $29.99. The first two listings do not show recurring plans; Kegel Morse Hero also offers subscriptions.

### Is a lifetime purchase the same as no subscription?

It avoids recurring billing if you choose that purchase, but the app may still sell subscription plans to other users. Confirm the product name and price in the store sheet before paying.

### What is the safest app after prostate surgery?

Use the timing and program given by your surgical or physiotherapy team. Squeezy Men is designed for clinician-configured routines, but an app does not replace postoperative advice.
