# Last Longer in Bed Exercise App: 5 Minutes a Day

A last longer in bed exercise app is, at its core, a coach for pelvic floor training: it tells you when to squeeze, how long to hold, and when to relax, and it reminds you to show up every day. That matters because the training only has a chance to help if you keep doing it for weeks. An app cannot measure your muscles or treat premature ejaculation.

> **Short answer:** A good app gives you quick squeezes, slow holds, real rest periods, discreet cues, and reminders, in about five minutes a day. Use it for consistency, and see a clinician if you have pain or if lasting time is a real source of distress.

## What a Last Longer in Bed Exercise App Can and Cannot Do

Small studies, most notably a 12-week supervised program by Pastore and colleagues, suggest pelvic floor rehabilitation may improve ejaculatory control for some men. Those programs included professional biofeedback; an app is a simpler tool.

**An app can:**

- Time contractions and rests so you do not rush them.
- Mix quick squeezes, longer holds, and relaxation in one session.
- Give discreet cues, such as vibration, so you can train anywhere.
- Remind you daily and show a history of completed sessions.

**An app cannot:**

- Tell whether you are contracting the right muscle.
- Detect an over-tight pelvic floor that needs relaxation rather than strength.
- Diagnose premature ejaculation, erectile dysfunction, or their causes.
- Promise a specific gain in minutes.

## What to Look for in an App

Use this checklist before you pay for anything:

1. **Both squeeze speeds.** Quick squeezes and slow holds train different qualities. See [Quick vs. Slow Kegels](https://kegelmorsehero.com/articles/quick-vs-slow-kegels/).
2. **Real rest.** Rest should be at least as long as the hold. Apps that chain squeezes with no recovery train fatigue, not control.
3. **Relaxation built in.** Letting go is part of ejaculatory control. Look for pauses or relaxation cues, and learn [reverse Kegels](https://kegelmorsehero.com/articles/reverse-kegels-for-men/) separately.
4. **Discreet cues.** Haptics or audio let you train with the screen off.
5. **Progression.** You should be able to increase difficulty gradually.
6. **Reminders and history.** Consistency is the main thing an app adds.
7. **Privacy.** Check whether an account, tracking, or ads are involved.
8. **Honest claims.** Skip apps promising a cure or a set number of minutes.

A simple five-minute session:

1. **Warm-up, 30 seconds.** Lie down or sit. Take five slow breaths and let the pelvic floor soften on each exhale.
2. **SOS at 1x, twice.** Three quick squeezes, three holds, three quick squeezes. Rest about 20 seconds between rounds.
3. **HERO at 1x, twice.** A longer mix of holds and quick squeezes. Keep effort steady, not maximal.
4. **KEGEL at 1x, once.** Focus on releasing fully in every gap.
5. **Cool-down, 30 seconds.** Five relaxed breaths, letting the pelvic floor drop gently.

## Who Should Not Rely on an App

Get assessed first if you have pelvic or genital pain, pain after ejaculation, difficulty starting urine, worsening urgency, or symptoms that get worse after sessions. Also see a clinician if short lasting time is new or comes with erection problems; a urologist can explain behavioral, therapy, and medical options.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there an app to help you last longer in bed?

Several apps guide pelvic floor exercises, which small studies suggest may help some men with ejaculatory control. No app is proven to make you last longer on its own.

### Is 5 minutes a day enough?

Short daily sessions are a reasonable starting point and are easier to keep up. There is no proven minimum, and supervised study programs ran for about 12 weeks.

### What is a good last longer in bed workout for men?

A balanced routine mixes slow holds, quick squeezes, and full relaxation, with rest at least as long as each hold. See our [last longer routine](https://kegelmorsehero.com/workouts/last-longer-routine-for-men/).
