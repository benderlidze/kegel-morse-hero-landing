# PC Muscle Exercises for Men: Find It and Train It

PC muscle exercises for men are simply Kegels: you lift and squeeze the muscles you would use to stop urine and hold in gas, then fully let go. Find the right muscle first, train slow holds and quick squeezes a few times a day, and practice relaxing as carefully as you practice squeezing.

> **Short answer:** Locate the muscle with a "stop the stream" or "hold in gas" test, then do slow holds, quick squeezes and full relaxation daily for at least several weeks. A PC muscle trainer can add feedback, but it is optional.

## What Is the PC Muscle?

"PC" stands for pubococcygeus. It is one part of the levator ani, the muscle sheet that runs from the pubic bone at the front to the tailbone at the back and supports the bladder and bowel.

In everyday use, "PC muscle" usually means the whole pelvic floor you feel when you squeeze. That is fine for training, because you cannot contract the pubococcygeus on its own anyway. Two smaller muscles near the base of the penis, the bulbocavernosus and ischiocavernosus, matter most for erections and ejaculation, and they work together with the deeper layer during a normal Kegel.

## How to Find Your PC Muscle

Try these checks once or twice, not as daily exercises:

1. **Gas test.** Imagine you are in a quiet room and need to hold in gas. The squeeze-and-lift you feel around the back passage is the pelvic floor.
2. **Urine test.** Midway through peeing, slow or stop the stream once. Do not make this a routine, because repeatedly interrupting urination is not recommended.
3. **Mirror test.** Standing, tighten as if stopping urine and gas. The base of the penis should draw slightly in and the testicles should rise.
4. **Hand check.** Place two fingers on the skin between the scrotum and anus. You should feel a gentle lift inward, not a push outward.

If your buttocks clench, your belly hardens or you hold your breath, you are using the wrong muscles. Training through pelvic pain is also a reason to stop and get assessed. ## 5 PC Muscle Exercises for Men

Start lying down with knees bent. It is the easiest position to feel the lift.

### 1. Slow holds (strength)

Squeeze and lift, hold for 3 to 5 seconds, then relax for the same time or longer. Build toward holds of up to about 10 seconds over several weeks. Stop the hold when form slips.

### 2. Quick squeezes (fast response)

Squeeze firmly for about one second, release completely, repeat 5 to 10 times. The difference between the two types is explained in [quick vs slow Kegels](https://kegelmorsehero.com/articles/quick-vs-slow-kegels/).

### 3. Half-strength lift (control)

Lift to roughly half of your maximum and hold it while breathing normally. In a well-known erectile dysfunction trial, men were asked to lift their pelvic floor about 50% while walking. This teaches control rather than maximal effort.

### 4. Functional squeeze (the habit)

Tighten just before you cough, sneeze or lift something heavy. After urinating, give one firm squeeze to clear the last drops.

### 5. Full relaxation drop (the reset)

After each set, let the pelvic floor drop and soften on a slow breath out. A tight, always-on pelvic floor can cause pain or urinary trouble, so relaxing is part of the exercise. See [reverse Kegels for men](https://kegelmorsehero.com/articles/reverse-kegels-for-men/) for a gentle version.

## How to Strengthen PC Muscles: A Weekly Plan

| Week | Position | Slow holds | Quick squeezes | Sessions per day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Lying | 5 × 3–5 s | 5 | 2 |
| 3–4 | Lying, sitting | 8 × 5–6 s | 8 | 2–3 |
| 5–8 | Sitting, standing | 10 × 6–8 s | 10 | 3 |
| 9+ | Standing, walking | 10 × up to 10 s | 10 | 3, plus functional squeezes |

Rest at least as long as you hold. Breathe throughout. If you want a sense of volume limits, read [how many Kegels a day for men](https://kegelmorsehero.com/articles/how-many-kegels-a-day-for-men/). Most official guidance says results take weeks to months, not days.

## Do PC Muscle Trainers Work?

A "PC muscle trainer" can mean three things:

- **Biofeedback devices.** Some, such as the kGoal Boost, are non-insertable pads you sit on that show how strongly you contract. Clinics use similar feedback; the best-known ED trial combined exercises with anal pressure biofeedback supervised by a physiotherapist. Feedback can help you learn the movement, but no device does the work for you.
- **Timer and guide apps.** They structure holds, rests and reminders. They do not measure anything.
- **Grippers, weights and "PC builders."** Be skeptical of products that promise size or dramatic results. Pelvic floor training is not shown to change penis size.

If you cannot feel any lift after a few weeks, a pelvic floor physical therapist is usually more useful than a gadget.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I strengthen my PC muscles fast?

There is no fast track. Consistent daily slow holds and quick squeezes with good form, progressed over weeks, beat high rep counts. Most men need several weeks before noticing a change.

### Are pubococcygeus muscle exercises for men different from Kegels?

No. Pubococcygeus exercises, PC muscle exercises and Kegels all train the same pelvic floor group.

### Do I need a PC muscle trainer?

No. A biofeedback device can help if you struggle to feel the muscle, but many men learn with body cues, a timer and, when needed, a physical therapist.

### What are the benefits of PC muscle exercises for men sexually?

Studies suggest pelvic floor training may help some men with erection firmness or ejaculatory control, especially in supervised programs. Results vary, and they are not guaranteed.
