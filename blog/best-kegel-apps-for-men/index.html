Best Kegel Apps for Men in 2026: An Honest Comparison

Reviewed August 4, 2026By Kegel Morse HeroComparison guide

Quick answer: Squeezy Men is the strongest clinical-program option; Kegel Trainer is a simple general timer; Kegel Morse Hero is designed for rhythm and adherence; Squeeze Time emphasizes customization; Dr. Kegel offers a broader subscription program.

Ownership disclosure: I am the developer of Kegel Morse Hero, one of the apps reviewed below. I have not ranked it first.

The best Kegel app depends on the problem you are trying to solve.

If a clinician has given you a specific rehabilitation program, choose an app that can reproduce it. If you already know the exercise but keep quitting, choose the app you will actually open after week two.

Features and US App Store listings were checked on August 4, 2026. Prices and subscription offers can change by country, promotion, and account.

1. Squeezy Men: Best for a Clinician-Set Program

Squeezy Men is the strongest choice when a pelvic floor physical therapist has already told you what to do.

It was designed by chartered NHS physiotherapists specializing in men’s health. The app supports:

  • Custom exercise plans.
  • A professional mode for clinician configuration.
  • Visual and audio prompts.
  • Progress records.
  • A bladder diary.
  • Notes after sessions.

The App Store listing says the app is UKCA marked as a Class I medical device in the United Kingdom.

Who should choose it?

Choose Squeezy Men if:

  • You are working with a pelvic floor physical therapist.
  • You are preparing for or recovering from prostate surgery.
  • You need to reproduce a prescribed hold, rest, and repetition structure.
  • You prefer a one-time purchase.

Who should skip it?

Skip it if your main problem is boredom and you need a more playful reason to return. Its clinical clarity is the feature, but also the personality.

2. Kegel Trainer PFM Exercises: Best Simple General Timer

Kegel Trainer PFM Exercises is a widely rated general pelvic floor timer.

Its App Store listing highlights:

  • Ten guided sessions.
  • Sessions lasting roughly 30 seconds to three minutes.
  • Daily reminders.
  • Achievements and streaks.
  • Visual, audio, or vibration cues.
  • A discreet app icon and name.

Who should choose it?

It works well for someone who wants a familiar timer without a large men’s wellness platform around it.

It is also useful when discretion matters. Vibration guidance lets you train without staring at the screen.

Who should skip it?

Skip it if you need a clinician to configure a detailed post-surgical program or if you specifically want male-focused education.

3. Kegel Morse Hero: Best for Making Repetition Less Boring

Kegel Morse Hero maps short contractions to Morse-code dits and longer holds to dahs.

That changes the delivery format, not the underlying exercise.

The current App Store listing shows that the app collects no user data. Its strengths are:

  • Rhythmic rather than purely countdown-based sessions.
  • Discreet haptic guidance.
  • Short and long contractions in memorable patterns.
  • Progress history.
  • A small, focused app rather than a broad lifestyle platform.

Its main weakness is clinical structure.

It is not the first choice when a physiotherapist has prescribed an exact rehabilitation plan. Squeezy Men is better for that.

Who should choose it?

Choose it if you understand the basic exercise but stop because standard timers become boring.

Who should skip it?

Skip it if you need biofeedback, diagnosis, post-operative supervision, or a clinician-authored program.

4. Squeeze Time for Men: Best for Customization and Statistics

Squeeze Time for Men combines guided programs with customization, statistics, reminders, and community features.

The US listing shows:

  • Free download.
  • A $4.99 Premium purchase.
  • Custom exercises.
  • Calendar and progress statistics.
  • Programs for different goals and levels.
  • Apple Watch support.
  • Social features called Squeeze Friends.

Who should choose it?

Choose it if you like adjusting routines and looking at progress data.

The one-time Premium price is also easier to understand than an ongoing subscription.

Who should skip it?

Recent App Store reviews report problems with the Watch app resetting or timing out. Check the latest reviews before choosing it mainly for Apple Watch use.

5. Dr. Kegel: Best for a Broad Structured Program

Dr. Kegel is the largest and most polished men’s wellness platform in this comparison.

Its listing includes:

  • A step-by-step Kegel plan.
  • Adaptive difficulty.
  • Vibration-guided exercises.
  • Progress tracking.
  • Additional groin exercises.
  • Mental wellness content.
  • Lifestyle challenges.
  • Educational stories.

The listing presents the product as a broad subscription program and repeatedly cites an in-app user survey. Treat that survey as marketing data, not clinical evidence.

Who should choose it?

Choose it if you want a broad program and do not mind paying for a subscription.

Who should skip it?

Skip it if you only need a clean contraction-and-rest timer. The app contains far more than pelvic floor timing, and its store copy makes strong performance claims that should not be treated as personal medical predictions.

What Criteria Matter More Than the App Store Rating?

Can You Follow the Routine Without Looking?

Haptics, audio cues, and clear phase changes matter more than decorative charts.

Can You Set the Correct Program?

After surgery or physical therapy, customization is more important than gamification.

Does the App Encourage Relaxation?

An app that only rewards longer, harder contractions can be a poor fit for someone with an overactive pelvic floor.

Is the Price Clear?

Check whether “free” means a usable free version, a trial, or only a download before a subscription screen.

Will You Still Use It in Two Weeks?

The best exercise plan has no effect when the app is abandoned.

Read How to Remember to Do Kegels Every Day for the habit system behind this problem.

When Is an App Not the Answer?

An app cannot:

  • Confirm that you contracted the correct muscle.
  • Diagnose weakness or overactivity.
  • Examine pain.
  • Assess surgical recovery.
  • Determine the cause of urinary or sexual symptoms.
  • Replace a pelvic floor physical therapist or urologist.

Get assessed if Kegels cause pain, urgency, hesitancy, incomplete emptying, or worsening sexual symptoms.

Read Can You Do Too Many Kegels? before choosing an app that mainly pushes higher volume.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free Kegel app for men?

Kegel Trainer PFM Exercises offers a usable free download with optional Pro features. Kegel Morse Hero is also free to download with a paid tier. Check the current paywall before committing because pricing can change.

What is the best Kegel app after prostate surgery?

Squeezy Men is the strongest fit in this list because it was designed by men’s health physiotherapists and can be configured for a clinician-set program. Follow your surgical team’s timing and instructions.

Do Kegel apps know whether I am squeezing correctly?

No. A phone timer can guide timing but cannot directly measure the pelvic floor. Hardware biofeedback and professional assessment can provide more information.

Are Kegel subscriptions worth it?

Only when the added structure or content keeps you consistent. A subscription is unnecessary if a simple timer and reminders solve the problem.
