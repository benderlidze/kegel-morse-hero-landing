Kegel Morse Hero

Features

Everything included in Kegel Morse Hero to support your pelvic floor training.

How does Kegel Morse Hero guide exercises?

A visual ring animation marks every squeeze and every relaxation. Each session follows a Morse code pattern: a dot is a short, quick squeeze, a dash is a longer hold, and the gaps between signals are your rest. Haptic feedback pulses in time with the pattern, so the timing stays clear even when you are not looking at the screen.

Why Morse code?

Pelvic floor training usually mixes quick squeezes, longer holds and full relaxation. Morse code already contains exactly that mix: short dots, long dashes and pauses. Turning a word into a rhythm gives each session a recognizable shape, which is easier to follow and less monotonous than silently counting seconds.

What Morse code tracks are included?

Built-in tracks include SOS (16 s), HELLO (26 s), HELP ME (28 s), HERO (20 s), KEGEL (22 s), MORSE (23 s), BRAVO (28 s), READY (24 s) and FOCUS (29 s). Short tracks such as SOS and HERO are a good place to start; longer ones add more holds and more repetitions.

Can I create my own tracks?

Yes. Type any word or short phrase and the app converts each letter into dots and dashes. Longer words make longer sessions, so start with a short word and build up. Custom tracks are a simple way to keep training fresh once the built-in ones feel familiar.

Can I train discreetly?

Yes. Haptic cues mean you do not need sound or a visible screen: you can keep the phone on a desk or in your hand and follow the vibration. A Kegel is invisible from the outside, so you can practice at a desk, while waiting in a queue or before sleep.

Can I adjust exercise speed?

Choose 1x, 2x or 3x speed. 1x gives the longest holds and rests and is the most forgiving place to start. Faster speeds shorten every signal and every pause, which demands quicker contractions and quicker relaxation, so move up only when the slower pace feels controlled.

What is Zen Mode?

Zen Mode removes non-essential interface elements and keeps only the timing guidance, so nothing distracts you during a session. It works well together with haptics for eyes-free training.

Can the app help with consistency?

Yes. Signal reminders can be set for daily or custom schedules. Consistency matters more than intensity for pelvic floor training, and a reminder at the same time every day is one of the easiest ways to keep the habit.

How does progress tracking work?

Every completed session is saved automatically in a calendar view and a history log, with the date, the track, the duration and the speed. The calendar makes gaps easy to spot, and the history shows how your sessions change over weeks.

How is user data handled?

All exercise data is stored on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Optional Google Drive backup and CSV export are available when you choose to use them; a backup goes to your own Google account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Exercise sessions, tracks, reminders and history all work without an internet connection.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Kegel Morse Hero is available for iPhone (iOS 17.2 or later) on the App Store and for Android on Google Play. The app is available in English, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian, Spanish and Ukrainian.

What the app does not do

The app guides timing; it does not measure muscle activity, because a phone has no sensor for that. If you are unsure whether you are contracting the right muscles, a pelvic floor physiotherapist can check. Kegel Morse Hero is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
