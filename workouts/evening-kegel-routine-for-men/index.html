Evening Kegel routine for men: 5 calm moves before bed

Slow, low-effort moves for the end of the day. Everything is done lying down or sitting on the floor, so you can do it right next to your bed.

BeginnerNo equipmentAt home7 minutes

Kegel (lying)

10 reps · 3 s hold

Pelvic floor

  2. Squeeze as if stopping your pee and lift up and in
  3. Relax fully between reps

How to do this routine

  • Do the moves in order, once through.
  • Rest about 30 seconds between moves.
  • Keep the lights low and the pace slow; stop each move before it feels like effort.
  • Three to four times a week is a good start; quality beats speed.
