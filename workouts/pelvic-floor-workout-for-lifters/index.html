Pelvic floor workout for lifters: before heavy squats

Lifters brace hard but rarely train the muscles at the bottom of the core. This routine warms up the breathing, hips and glutes before heavy squats or deadlifts.

IntermediateNo equipmentAt home9 minutes

Common mistake: Lower back lifting off the floor. Instead: Press your back down and move slower.

How to do this routine

  • Do the moves in order, once through.
  • Rest about 30 seconds between moves.
  • Use it as a warm-up before leg day. Start with the breathing to practise bracing and releasing.
  • Three to four times a week is a good start; quality beats speed.
