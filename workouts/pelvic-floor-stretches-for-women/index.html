Belly Breathing

Slow breaths into the belly

1–2 min

WorksDiaphragmPelvic floor

  1. One hand on your belly
  2. Inhale: belly rises, floor relaxes
  3. Exhale: gentle lift

Let the pelvic floor soften on the inhale. This is as important as the squeeze.

How to do this routine

  • Do the moves in order, once through.
  • Rest about 30 seconds between moves.
  • Hold each stretch for 30-60 seconds and never push into pain.
  • Three to four times a week is a good start; quality beats speed.
