6

Kegel (lying)

Squeeze, hold, relax

10 reps · 3 s hold

WorksPelvic floor

  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat
  2. Squeeze as if stopping your pee and lift up and in
  3. Relax fully between reps

Breathe normally. If your belly, glutes or thighs tense up, the squeeze is too hard: go lighter.

How to do this routine

  • Do the moves in order, once through.
  • Rest about 30 seconds between moves.
  • Start with one round. Drop to your knees on the plank if you need to.
  • Three to four times a week is a good start; quality beats speed.
