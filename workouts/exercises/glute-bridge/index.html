Glute Bridge: how to do it (with pictures)

Lift hips, squeeze at the top.

WorksGlutesHamstringsPelvic floor

The glute bridge strengthens the glutes and hamstrings around the back of the pelvis. It does not train the pelvic floor directly, but strong hips support the pelvis, and a gentle Kegel as you lift links the two.

How to do it

  1. Feet flat, hip-width apart
  2. Exhale, squeeze and lift your hips
  3. Lower slowly

12 reps

How to know it is working

You should feel your buttocks and the back of your thighs working, with a line from knees to shoulders at the top. Your lower back feels neutral, not pinched. Watch for breath holding, pressing your head down or hamstring cramps. If you feel it mostly in your lower back, lift a little lower and squeeze your glutes more.

Common mistakes

  • Pushing through the toes. Press through your heels. If your hamstrings cramp, bring your feet a little closer to your hips.
  • Knees falling in or out. Keep knees in line with hips and feet on the way up and down.
  • Dropping down fast. Lower slowly, one vertebra at a time, and relax fully at the bottom.

Make it easier or harder

Easier: Lift only halfway, or do fewer reps with a short rest between each.

Harder: Hold the top for 3 to 5 seconds, slow the lowering, or move on to the bridge march or single-leg bridge.

Who it helps

Desk workers with weak or stiff hips, beginners, and people over 40 who want hip strength for daily movement.

Skip it or get checked first if

Modify or skip after recent back, hip or abdominal surgery, with a hernia, or if back pain worsens with the lift. In later pregnancy, lying flat may be uncomfortable, so ask your doctor or physiotherapist. Stop if it hurts.

Questions

Do glute bridges strengthen the pelvic floor?

Bridges mainly work the glutes and hamstrings. The pelvic floor joins in a little, and you can add a gentle Kegel at the top, but keep doing Kegels too.

Why do I feel bridges in my lower back?

Usually the back arches at the top or the glutes are not doing enough. Keep your ribs down, lift a little lower and squeeze your buttocks.
