Cat-Cow: how to do it (with pictures)

Round up, then arch down.

WorksSpineDeep core

Cat-cow is a slow spinal movement on hands and knees, timed with your breath. In a pelvic floor routine its main job is relaxation and mobility, not strength. It helps you feel the pelvic floor lift and let go with the breath, which matters if your muscles tend to stay tight.

How to do it

  1. Start on hands and knees
  2. Exhale: round your back up
  3. Inhale: drop your belly, look up

8 slow rounds

Move slowly and match the movement to your breath.

How to know it is working

You should feel your spine move segment by segment, with a mild stretch in the back as you round and in the front as you arch. You should not feel pinching in the lower back, neck strain or rushed breathing. As you inhale and drop the belly, notice the pelvic floor soften and widen. If the movement feels jerky, make it smaller and slower.

Common mistakes

  • Moving only the neck and head. Start from the tailbone and let the movement travel up the spine. The head follows last.
  • Forcing a deep arch in the lower back. Keep the arch comfortable. The aim is easy movement, not end range.
  • Breathing out of sync with the movement. Exhale as you round up, inhale as the belly drops, and let the breath set the pace.

Make it easier or harder

Easier: Do it seated on a chair with hands on your knees, or pad your knees if kneeling is uncomfortable.

Harder: Slow each round to five or six seconds, or add a gentle pelvic floor lift on the exhale and a full release on the inhale.

Who it helps

Great for desk workers and drivers who sit for hours, anyone stiff in the morning, and people whose pelvic floor feels tense or overactive.

Skip it or get checked first if

If your wrists hurt, use fists or forearms or try the seated version. Check with a doctor or physiotherapist first if you have acute back pain or recent spinal, abdominal or pelvic surgery. Stop if it hurts.

Questions

Does cat-cow help pelvic floor relaxation?

It can. Pairing the arch with a slow inhale helps many people feel the pelvic floor let go, which is useful if it tends to stay clenched.

Is cat-cow safe during pregnancy?

It is commonly used in pregnancy because it can ease back pressure. Ask your midwife or doctor if you have any complications.
