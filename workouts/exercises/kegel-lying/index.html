Kegel (lying): how to do it (with pictures)

Squeeze, hold, relax.

WorksPelvic floor

The lying Kegel is the starting point for pelvic-floor training. On your back, gravity is not pressing down on the pelvic floor, so the muscles have the least load to lift and you can focus on finding them.

How to do it

  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat
  2. Squeeze as if stopping your pee and lift up and in
  3. Relax fully between reps

10 reps · 3 s hold

Breathe normally. If your belly, glutes or thighs tense up, the squeeze is too hard: go lighter.

How to know it is working

You should feel a small lift inside the pelvis, then a clear letting go. Your belly, glutes and thighs stay soft and your breath keeps flowing. Pushing down, a held breath or a tight jaw mean you should ease off. Self-check: a hand on your lower belly may feel a slight tightening, but no hard bracing or bulging.

Common mistakes

  • Squeezing the glutes or thighs instead. Keep your seat on the floor and your knees still. If your hips lift or knees press together, other muscles took over.
  • Skipping the relaxation between reps. Rest at least as long as you held. The full release is part of the exercise.
  • Adding extra sets because it feels easy. More is not better. Add time only once every hold stays controlled to the last second.

Make it easier or harder

Easier: Hold for 1 to 2 seconds and do fewer reps. A pillow under your knees helps your belly and back relax.

Harder: Build up to longer holds with equal rest, or add a few quick squeezes after the slow ones.

Who it helps

Anyone new to Kegels, people unsure whether they are squeezing correctly, and people returning after a break or childbirth once cleared.

Skip it or get checked first if

With pelvic pain, pain with sex or a pelvic floor that feels tight, more squeezing may not help; start with breathing and see a pelvic floor physiotherapist. After pelvic or abdominal surgery or a recent birth, ask your doctor when to begin. Stop if it hurts.

Questions

Why start Kegels lying down?

Lying down takes the weight of your organs off the pelvic floor, so it can lift more easily. It is also easier to notice when other muscles join in.

How do I know I am doing a Kegel correctly?

You feel a lift and squeeze inside, then a release, without holding your breath. Men may notice a slight lift of the penis and testicles; women may feel the vaginal opening gently tighten.
