Pelvic floor exercises for long drives: a routine for drivers

One move for the driver seat and four for your next rest stop, to loosen hips and lower back after hours of sitting.

Kegel (sitting)

10 reps · 5 s hold

WorksPelvic floor

  1. Sit tall, feet flat
  2. Squeeze and lift, keep glutes soft
  3. Breathe normally

Do it on a chair, in a car or at a desk: nobody can see it. Keep your shoulders loose.

Hip Flexor Stretch

Kneel and shift forward

30 s each side

WorksHip flexors

  1. Half-kneel, back knee down
  2. Squeeze the back glute
  3. Shift hips forward gently
3

Bodyweight Squat

Lower down, rise back up

12 reps

WorksGlutesThighs

  1. Feet shoulder-width apart
  2. Sit back, chest up
  3. Exhale and lift as you stand

Common mistake: Rounding your back. Instead: Chest up, back long.

4

Cat-Cow

Round up, then arch down

8 slow rounds

WorksSpineDeep core

  1. Start on hands and knees
  2. Exhale: round your back up
  3. Inhale: drop your belly, look up

Move slowly and match the movement to your breath.

5

Child's Pose

Sit back, arms long

30–60 s

WorksLower backHips

  1. Knees wide, big toes together
  2. Sit back on your heels
  3. Breathe into your lower back

Let your belly and pelvic floor fully relax here. Do not push into any pain.

How to do this routine

  • Do the moves in order, once through.
  • Rest about 30 seconds between moves.
  • Only do the seated Kegel while parked or as a passenger; do the rest at a stop.
  • Three to four times a week is a good start; quality beats speed.
