1

Kegel (lying)

Squeeze, hold, relax

10 reps · 3 s hold

WorksPelvic floor

  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat
  2. Squeeze as if stopping your pee and lift up and in
  3. Relax fully between reps

Breathe normally. If your belly, glutes or thighs tense up, the squeeze is too hard: go lighter.

2

Glute Bridge

Lift hips, squeeze at the top

12 reps

WorksGlutesHamstringsPelvic floor

  1. Feet flat, hip-width apart
  2. Exhale, squeeze and lift your hips
  3. Lower slowly

Common mistake: Arching your lower back at the top. Instead: Ribs down, straight line from knees to shoulders.

Clamshell

12 each side

WorksSide glutesHips

  2. Feet together, lift the top knee
  3. Don't roll your hips back

5

Dead Bug

Lower opposite arm and leg

8 each side

WorksDeep abdominals

  1. Arms up, knees over hips
  2. Lower opposite arm and leg
  3. Lower back stays on the floor

Common mistake: Lower back lifting off the floor. Instead: Press your back down and move slower.

6

Bodyweight Squat

Lower down, rise back up

12 reps

WorksGlutesThighs

  1. Feet shoulder-width apart
  2. Sit back, chest up
  3. Exhale and lift as you stand

Common mistake: Rounding your back. Instead: Chest up, back long.

How to do this routine

  • Do the moves in order, once through.
  • Rest about 30 seconds between moves.
  • Start with one round. When it feels easy, do two rounds.
  • Three to four times a week is a good start; quality beats speed.
