Pelvic floor exercises for women runners

Strong glutes and hips help keep the pelvis steady with every stride. This routine trains them and then stretches the hips.

IntermediateNo equipmentAt home9 minutes

The hardest position because gravity works against you. Master the lying version first.

How to do this routine

  • Do the moves in order, once through.
  • Rest about 30 seconds between moves.
  • Do it on easy days or after a run, not right before a hard session.
  • Three to four times a week is a good start; quality beats speed.
